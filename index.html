Who am I?

Hi, I'm Adrian. I just finished my education at NTNU taking a bachelor's degree in Web Development. I've always had a fascination for electronics, both the physical hardware and the software that runs on it. That's what led me to my degree, as I feel like it teaches a lot about both. In my time at NTNU I've learned about databases, security, different code languages, and even built electronic devices from scratch, which I'd like to showcase further down. Since the degree is part of the Institue for Design, I've also had courses teaching me to be more than just a coder, and to create with intention. While my interest primarily align with electronics, learning design concepts such as picking a shade of color that resembles solid black or white to portray more depth, flavor and personality have been instrumental in shaping how I approach creation today.

Previous work experience

I previously spent 3 years working as a lab assistant in Oslo, where I spent my day helping bio-chemcial engineers and scientists with their work. I would do anything that did not require a bio-chemical degree, so I am used to flexible work tasks. I would mostly organize logistics and sort blood samples, as that was the primary workload. In my time there I learned to see the value of many different tasks, and be flexible in what I spend my day doing. I also learned teamwork, and how to work with many different people from different backgrounds and expriences. I value that as the time I learned who I am as a person, which is what led me to pursue a degree in a field I am passionate about, namely electronics. I wanted a degree that gave the same level of flexibility, while providing a solid core in the IT field, which is what I found at NTNU's course in Web Development.
